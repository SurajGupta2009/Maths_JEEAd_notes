Solution

Method 1: factor by roots. The roots sum to \(5\) and multiply to \(6\), so they are \(2\) and \(3\): \[x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x-2)(x-3) = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; x \in \{2, 3\}.\]

Method 2: quadratic formula. \[x = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{25-24}}{2} = \frac{5 \pm 1}{2} \in \{2, 3\}.\]

Answer: \(x = 2,\ 3\)

Check: \(2+3 = 5\) ✓ and \(2 \\cdot 3 = 6\) ✓ — and both satisfy the original equation. (Both methods agree, which is the point of showing two.)
