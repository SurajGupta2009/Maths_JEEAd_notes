PRINTING THE SHEETS — Chapter 6: Quadratic Equations
======================================================

Sheets are 2480 x 3508 px portrait PNGs with nominal 300 DPI metadata
(A4 aspect). Print one sheet per page.

From the PDFs (recommended)
---------------------------
  exports/06-quadratic-equations-combined.pdf   (both parts, JEE first)
  exports/06-quadratic-equations-JEE.pdf        (Part I only)
  exports/06-quadratic-equations-Olympiad-extensions.pdf (Part II only)

  Paper size: A4, portrait, one sheet per page.
  Scaling: "Fit" / "Fit to printable area" — NOT "Fill", NOT "Actual size",
           NOT "Crop". Choose "Auto portrait/landscape" or force Portrait.

From the PNGs
-------------
  exports/06-quadratic-equations-combined-sheets.zip
  (or -JEE-sheets.zip / -Olympiad-sheets.zip for one part)

  Print order: JEE/01.png ... JEE/08.png, then Olympiad/01.png ... 03.png.
  In the print dialog: A4 portrait, one page per sheet, scale to fit
  printable area (do not crop; the sheets carry their own ~10 mm safe margins).

Bookmarks
---------
  Each PDF has outline bookmarks per part and per sheet title;
  the combined PDF lists Part I (JEE) before Part II (extensions).
