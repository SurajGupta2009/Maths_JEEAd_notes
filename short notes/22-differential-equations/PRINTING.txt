PRINTING — Chapter 22: Differential Equations
============================================

Sheets: 2480 × 3508 px portrait, nominal 300 DPI, A4 aspect. Print one PNG
sheet on each A4 portrait page.

Recommended: exports/22-differential-equations-combined.pdf
(30 JEE sheets first, then 12 Olympiad/extension sheets). Separate PDFs:
  exports/22-differential-equations-JEE.pdf
  exports/22-differential-equations-Olympiad-extensions.pdf

Print A4 portrait, one sheet per page, scaling Fit or Fit to printable area.
Do not use Fill, Crop, or Actual Size if it clips the margins. PNG ZIPs are
*-JEE-sheets.zip, *-Olympiad-sheets.zip and *-combined-sheets.zip. PDFs carry
bookmarks by sheet. Read straight down each sheet and follow the lesson,
section and continuation ribbons; there are no column-based reading steps.
