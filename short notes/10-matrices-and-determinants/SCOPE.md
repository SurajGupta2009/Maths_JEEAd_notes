# Scope — Chapter 10: Matrices & Determinants

## Part I — JEE core and close applications (4 PNG sheets)

- Matrix dimensions, entries, row/column action, composition, addition,
  multiplication and noncommutativity; identity, powers, transpose, trace
  (as a useful derived quantity), symmetric/skew split.
- Determinants for 2×2 and 3×3; geometric signed-area/volume intuition,
  minors/cofactors, cofactor expansion, elementary row/column effects,
  singularity, multiplicativity, transpose and scalar identities.
- Adjugate as transpose of cofactor matrix, inverse iff determinant is
  nonzero, inverse identities; solving $AX=B$ and Cramer's rule for 2/3
  variables; homogeneous nontrivial-solution criterion and rank consistency
  explanation.
- Symmetric, skew-symmetric, orthogonal (length preserving), idempotent,
  involutory, nilpotent examples.

Official syllabus anchor: matrices and determinants, determinants up to order
3, adjoint/inverse, Cramer's rule for 2- and 3-variable systems. Row-reduction
and rank are explanatory tools for systems, not a claim of an extra syllabus
unit.

## Part II — Olympiad/specialised extensions (2 PNG sheets)

Characteristic polynomial, eigenvalues and similarity; Cayley–Hamilton;
Hadamard determinant bound; rank-one determinant lemma; Vandermonde;
trace-cyclicity, rank–nullity, block determinants and Schur complement.

## Assumptions

Matrices are over $\mathbb R$ in Part I unless stated. In the eigenvalue sheet,
diagonalisation is over a field containing all eigenvalues (at least $\mathbb C$
for a real matrix). The matrix determinant lemma requires invertible $A$;
Schur complement requires invertible leading block; equality conditions are
stated with the zero-column case handled separately.
