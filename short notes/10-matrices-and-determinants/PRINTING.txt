PRINTING — Chapter 10: Matrices & Determinants
==============================================

Sheets: 2480 × 3508 px portrait, nominal 300 DPI, A4 aspect. Print exactly
one PNG sheet on each A4 portrait page.

Recommended: use exports/10-matrices-and-determinants-combined.pdf
(JEE sheets first, followed by Olympiad extensions). Separate PDFs:
  exports/10-matrices-and-determinants-JEE.pdf
  exports/10-matrices-and-determinants-Olympiad-extensions.pdf

Print settings: A4, Portrait, one page per sheet, scaling “Fit” or “Fit to
printable area”. Do NOT use Fill, Crop, or Actual Size if it clips the margins.
The PNG ZIPs are named *-JEE-sheets.zip, *-Olympiad-sheets.zip, and
*-combined-sheets.zip. PDFs contain bookmarks by part and sheet.
