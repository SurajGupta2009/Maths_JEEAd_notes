## A · Ratios and identities (Q1–Q5)

#### **Q1**[JEE Main][identity]Verify $\sin^2 30^\circ+\cos^2 30^\circ=1$.

**Answer:** $=1$

#### **Q2**[JEE Main][standard values]Evaluate $\tan45^\circ$.

**Answer:** $1$

#### **Q3**[JEE Main][allied]Evaluate $\sin135^\circ$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\approx0.7071$

#### **Q4**[JEE Main][standard values]Evaluate $\sin75^\circ$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}\approx0.9659$

#### **Q5**[JEE Main][allied]Evaluate $\cos225^\circ$.

**Answer:** $-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\approx-0.7071$

## B · Compound angles (Q6–Q10)

#### **Q6**[JEE Main][compound]Evaluate $\tan(45^\circ+60^\circ)$.

**Answer:** $-(2+\sqrt3)\approx-3.7321$

#### **Q7**[JEE Main][compound]Verify $\cos(60^\circ-30^\circ)=\cos30^\circ$ using the formula.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$

#### **Q8**[JEE Main][compound]If $\sin A=\frac35$ and $\cos B=\frac5{13}$ with $A,B$ acute, find $\sin(A+B)$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{63}{65}$

#### **Q9**[JEE Adv][compound]If $\cos A=\frac35$ with $A$ acute, find $\cos2A$ and $\sin2A$.

**Answer:** $\cos2A=-\dfrac7{25}$, $\sin2A=\dfrac{24}{25}$

#### **Q10**[JEE Main][compound]Find $\tan(A-B)$ if $\tan A=\frac12$ and $\tan B=\frac13$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac17$

## C · Multiple and submultiple angles (Q11–Q15)

#### **Q11**[JEE Main][triple]Verify $\sin90^\circ=3\sin30^\circ-4\sin^3 30^\circ$.

**Answer:** $=1$ ✓

#### **Q12**[JEE Main][triple]Verify $\cos90^\circ=4\cos^3 30^\circ-3\cos30^\circ$.

**Answer:** $=0$ ✓

#### **Q13**[JEE Main][double]Verify $\tan60^\circ=\dfrac{2\tan30^\circ}{1-\tan^2 30^\circ}$.

**Answer:** $=\sqrt3$ ✓

#### **Q14**[JEE Main][half angle]Verify $\sin30^\circ=\sqrt{\dfrac{1-\cos60^\circ}{2}}$.

**Answer:** $=\dfrac12$ ✓

#### **Q15**[JEE Main][double angle]Verify $1+\cos A=2\cos^2\frac A2$ for $A=60^\circ$.

**Answer:** $1.5=1.5$ ✓

## D · Transformation formulae and sums (Q16–Q20)

#### **Q16**[JEE Main][sum to product]Evaluate $\sin75^\circ+\sin15^\circ$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{\sqrt6}{2}\approx1.2247$

#### **Q17**[JEE Main][difference of cosines]Evaluate $\cos75^\circ-\cos15^\circ$.

**Answer:** $-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\approx-0.7071$

#### **Q18**[JEE Main][product to sum]Express $2\sin45^\circ\cos30^\circ$ as a sum of sines.

**Answer:** $\sin75^\circ+\sin15^\circ$

#### **Q19**[JEE Adv][product to sum]Express $\sin50^\circ\cos20^\circ$ as a sum.

**Answer:** $\frac12(\sin70^\circ+\sin30^\circ)$

#### **Q20**[Olympiad][series]Find $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}\sin(k\theta)$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{\sin\frac{n\theta}{2}\sin\frac{(n+1)\theta}{2}}{\sin\frac\theta2}$

## E · Trigonometric equations (Q21–Q25)

#### **Q21**[JEE Main][general]Solve $\sin x=\frac12$ generally.

**Answer:** $x=n\pi+(-1)^n\dfrac\pi6$

#### **Q22**[JEE Main][general]Solve $\cos x=\frac12$ generally.

**Answer:** $x=2n\pi\pm\dfrac\pi3$

#### **Q23**[JEE Main][general]Solve $\tan x=1$ generally.

**Answer:** $x=n\pi+\dfrac\pi4$

#### **Q24**[JEE Main][count]How many solutions does $\sin x=\frac12$ have in $[0,2\pi)$?

**Answer:** $2$

#### **Q25**[JEE Adv][factorise]Solve $\sin2x=\cos x$.

**Answer:** $x=\dfrac\pi2+n\pi$ or $x=n\pi+(-1)^n\dfrac\pi6$ (four solutions in $[0,2\pi)$)

## F · Properties of triangles (Q26–Q30)

#### **Q26**[JEE Main][sine rule]For $a=3$, $b=4$, $C=90^\circ$, find $c$, $R$ and the area.

**Answer:** $c=5$, $R=2.5$, area $6$

#### **Q27**[JEE Adv][half angle]For the same $3$-$4$-$5$ triangle, find $\tan\frac A2$ where $A$ is opposite $a=3$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac13$

#### **Q28**[JEE Main][cosine rule]In a triangle with $b=4$, $c=3$ and $A=90^\circ$, verify the cosine rule.

**Answer:** $a^2=16+9-0=25$, so $a=5$ ✓

#### **Q29**[JEE Main][area]Find the area of a triangle with $b=5$, $c=7$, $A=60^\circ$.

**Answer:** $\frac12\cdot5\cdot7\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{2}=\dfrac{35\sqrt3}{4}\approx15.1554$

#### **Q30**[JEE Adv][sine rule]In a triangle $a:b:c=3:4:5$. Find $R$ if the perimeter is $36$.

**Answer:** sides $9,12,15$; $R=\frac{15}{2}=7.5$

## G · Olympiad frontier (Q31–Q36)

#### **Q31**[Olympiad][special angles]Verify $\sin18^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}{4}$ and $\cos36^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt5+1}{4}$.

**Answer:** both verified

#### **Q32**[Olympiad][triangle identity]If $A+B+C=180^\circ$, prove $\sin2A+\sin2B+\sin2C=4\sin A\sin B\sin C$.

**Answer:** proved

#### **Q33**[Olympiad][triangle identity]If $A+B+C=180^\circ$, prove $\sin^2A+\sin^2B+\sin^2C=2+2\cos A\cos B\cos C$.

**Answer:** proved

#### **Q34**[Olympiad][triangle identity]Use $\tan3\theta=\dfrac{3t-t^3}{1-3t^2}$ with $t=\tan\theta$ to show that $\tan20^\circ\tan40^\circ\tan80^\circ=\sqrt3$.

**Answer:** $\sqrt3$

#### **Q35**[Olympiad][product]Prove that $\displaystyle\prod_{k=1}^{n-1}\sin\frac{k\pi}{n}=\frac{n}{2^{n-1}}$, and evaluate it for $n=7$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{n}{2^{n-1}}$; for $n=7$ it is $\dfrac7{64}$

#### **Q36**[Olympiad][series]Find $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{90}\sin(k^\circ)$ in closed form, and evaluate it.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{\sin45^\circ\sin45.5^\circ}{\sin0.5^\circ}\approx57.7943$

> [!note] Exam technique notes
> - **Reduce to one angle first.** In any triangle problem, replace $C$ by
>   $180^\circ-A-B$ and expand; almost every identity then falls out.
> - For a product of sines at multiples of $\frac\pi n$, think **roots of unity**:
>   factor $z^n-1$ and take moduli.
> - For a sum of $\cot^2$ or $\csc^2$ at multiples of $\frac\pi n$, think
>   **polynomial whose roots are the tangents**, then read off $\sum t_k^2$.
> - $\tan A+\tan B+\tan C=\tan A\tan B\tan C$ is the workhorse for symmetric
>   tangent expressions; check for a $90^\circ$ angle first.
