## A · Arithmetic progressions (Q1–Q5)

#### **Q1**[JEE Main][ap]For the AP $2,5,8,\ldots$, find the $20^{\rm th}$ term.

**Answer:** $59$

#### **Q2**[JEE Main][ap]Find the sum of the first $20$ terms of $2,5,8,\ldots$

**Answer:** $610$

#### **Q3**[JEE Main][ap]Find the common difference of $2,5,8,11,\ldots$

**Answer:** $3$

#### **Q4**[JEE Main][insert am]Insert three arithmetic means between $2$ and $14$.

**Answer:** $5,8,11$

#### **Q5**[JEE Main][ap]Find the sum of the first $20$ positive even integers.

**Answer:** $420$

## E · Telescoping and the method of differences (Q20–Q23)

#### **Q20**[JEE Main][telescoping]Evaluate $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{10}\frac1{k(k+1)}$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{10}{11}$

#### **Q21**[JEE Adv][telescoping]Evaluate $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{5}\frac1{k(k+2)}$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{25}{42}$

#### **Q22**[JEE Adv][telescoping]Evaluate $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac1{k(k+1)}$ in closed form.

**Answer:** $\dfrac n{n+1}$

#### **Q23**[JEE Adv][telescoping]Evaluate $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{3}\left(\frac1k-\frac1{k+1}\right)$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac34$

## G · Olympiad frontier (Q28–Q34)

#### **Q28**[JEE Adv][recurrence]Solve $a_n=2a_{n-1}+1$ with $a_1=1$.

**Answer:** $a_n=2^n-1$

#### **Q29**[Olympiad][shifted sum]Evaluate $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}k\,2^{k-1}$ in closed form.

**Answer:** $1+(n-1)2^n$

#### **Q30**[JEE Adv][amgm]Verify AM–GM for the first four positive integers.

**Answer:** AM $=2.5\ge$ GM $=24^{1/4}\approx2.2134$

#### **Q31**[JEE Main][gp]Find the common ratio of the GP obtained by inserting three geometric means between $1$ and $81$.

**Answer:** $3$

#### **Q32**[JEE Main][gp]Evaluate $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{10}2^k$.

**Answer:** $2047$

#### **Q33**[Olympiad][generating]Find the generating function of the constant sequence $1,1,1,\ldots$

**Answer:** $\dfrac1{1-x}$

#### **Q34**[Olympiad][series]Evaluate $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{5}\frac1{2k-1}$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{563}{315}\approx1.7873$

> [!note] Exam technique notes
> - Identify the family first: constant **difference** ⇒ AP, constant **ratio**
>   ⇒ GP, reciprocals in AP ⇒ HP. Test both difference and ratio before
>   concluding.
> - For AGPs and shifted sums, **write $S$, multiply by the ratio, subtract** —
>   do not try to memorise a formula per family.
> - For a rational general term, look for a product of terms in arithmetic
>   progression in the denominator: that is the signature of a telescoping sum.
> - Always check convergence ($\lvert r\rvert<1$) before using an infinite sum.
