# Scope — Chapter 6: Quadratic Equations

Official basis: JEE (Advanced) 2026 syllabus, Algebra — "quadratic equations
with real coefficients" (plus the complex-number-system strand shared with
chapter 5).

## In Part I (JEE core) — 8 sheets

- Standard form, quadratic formula **derived** by completing the square,
  discriminant cases with the parabola picture, conjugate root pairs
  (irrational $p \pm \sqrt{q}$ and complex $\alpha \pm i\beta$), rationality
  iff $D$ is a perfect square.
- Vieta's relations and symmetric functions: $\alpha^{2}+\beta^{2}$,
  $\alpha^{3}+\beta^{3}$, $\alpha^{2}\beta+\alpha\beta^{2}$, reciprocals,
  $(\alpha-\beta)^{2} = D/a^{2}$; forming equations from roots.
- The expression: completed-square form, vertex/extremum, sign analysis,
  always-$\geq 0$ conditions ($a>0$, $D\leq 0$), range on $\mathbb{R}$ and
  on intervals, AM–GM as a disguised square.
- **Location of roots** (a full sheet): both roots $>k$ / $<k$, $k$ between
  roots, both in an interval, exactly one in an interval, opposite signs —
  each as the three ingredients $D \geq 0$, $a f(k)$, vertex position, with
  strict/non-strict endpoint discipline.
- Reducible equations: biquadratic, reciprocal ($t = x + 1/x$,
  $|t| \geq 2$), exponential ($t = a^{x} > 0$), factor-pairing, radical
  equations with mandatory verification, lost-root traps.
- Common roots (subtract first; the elimination condition stated),
  proportional-coefficient case, transformed roots (shift, reciprocal,
  scale, squares, cubes) by new-$S$/new-$P$ and by substitution shortcuts.
- Parameter shapes (nature / root conditions / sign / location / range),
  discriminant method for ranges of rational functions, recap card,
  ten-mistake checklist.

## In Part II (Olympiad extensions) — 3 sheets

- Newton's recurrence $aS_{n} + bS_{n-1} + cS_{n-2} = 0$; power sums;
  $\alpha^{n} = F_{n}\alpha + F_{n-1}$ for $x^{2}-x-1$; Lucas numbers; Binet.
- Discriminant as $a^{2}(\alpha-\beta)^{2}$; rational-root criterion;
  depressed cubic and Lagrange's resolvent quadratic; why the radical
  ladder stops at degree 5 (Abel–Ruffini, stated as context).
- Vieta jumping: the root-flip descent, IMO 1988/6 with proof sketch,
  traced descent $(30,8) \to (8,2) \to (0,2)$, the $k=9$ solution chain.

## Out of scope

- General polynomial theory beyond quadratics (chapter 6 of the source
  module's cubic material appears only as the resolvent extension).
- Quadratic inequalities as a separate calculus topic (sign analysis is
  covered as part of the expression sheet).
- Coordinate-geometry uses of the parabola (chapters 12–14).
