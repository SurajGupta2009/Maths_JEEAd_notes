# Scope — Chapter 14: Conic Sections

**Official JEE Advanced 2026 anchor (Analytical Geometry, 2D):** equations of a parabola, ellipse and hyperbola in standard form; their foci, directrices and eccentricity; parametric equations; equations of tangent and normal; locus problems.

Official syllabus PDF: <https://jeeadv.ac.in/documents/jee-advanced-2026-syllabus.pdf>.

## Part I — JEE core and close applications

- Focus–directrix definition, eccentricity and the standard conic family; the circle as the $e=0$ limiting ellipse and the separate degenerate-locus caution.
- Parabola anatomy, orientations and vertex shifts, $P(t)=(at^2,2at)$, focal distances, chord and focal-chord conditions, tangent/normal forms, normal cubic, subnormal and reflection.
- Ellipse focal-sum definition, standard anatomy, focal distances, auxiliary circle and eccentric angle, point/parametric/slope tangents, parametric normal, position test, focal chords and director circle.
- Hyperbola absolute focal-difference definition, branches, focal distances, asymptotes, conjugate hyperbola, point/parametric/slope tangents, normal, director-locus reality cases and rectangular form $xy=c^2$.
- Unified $S,S_1,T$ conventions for tangents, chords of contact, pairs of tangents and midpoint chords; pole, polar and La Hire incidence as direct applications.
- Six visual-method sheets integrating the formulas with exact geometric configurations and a mixed-conic selection workflow.

Chord-of-contact, midpoint-chord, director-locus and pole–polar methods are useful derivable applications; they are not presented as separate verbatim bullets in the official conic sentence. The sheet count is reported in the README after build.

## Part II — Olympiad / specialised extensions

- Reflection laws and orthogonal confocal conics.
- Rectangular-hyperbola parametrization, the orthocentre theorem and the connection to general nine-point-circle facts.
- General second-degree equations, determinant/discriminant classification, rotation, degeneracy, and statement-level Pascal/Brianchon duality.

These are topic-linked extensions, not a complete IOQM/RMO course. Poncelet porism, isogonal conjugates, Carnot geometry and other material in the vault's broader frontier are not needed for this short-note chapter's stated extension scope.

## Conventions and hypotheses

- Horizontal central-conic formulas use $a>b>0$ for an ellipse and $c>a>0$ for a hyperbola. Reorient the coordinate roles for a vertical major/transverse axis.
- Tangent/normal-at-point formulas require the point to lie on the conic. Finite-slope formulas exclude vertical lines; handle those separately.
- A parabola normal at $t=0$ is the axis itself; the unique axis-intercept formula is stated for $t\ne0$.
- A finite-slope hyperbola tangent satisfies $|m|>b/a$; its vertex tangents are vertical.
- The hyperbola director locus $x^2+y^2=a^2-b^2$ is a real circle only for $a>b$; the rectangular case has no real perpendicular tangent pair.
- The unsigned focal-chord reciprocal rule for a hyperbola is restricted to same-branch chords with the focus between the endpoints. Opposite-branch chords require separate signs; the transverse-axis counterexample is included.
- Midpoint and chord formulas are checked for real endpoints. A midpoint on the conic is the coalesced/tangent boundary case.
- General-conic classification preserves the coefficients $2hxy,2gx,2fy$ and treats $\Delta=0$ as a degenerate case requiring factorization or completion of squares.
