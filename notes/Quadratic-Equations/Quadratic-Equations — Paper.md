## A · Roots and the discriminant (Q1–Q5)

#### **Q1**[JEE Main][roots]Solve $x^2-5x+6=0$.

**Answer:** $x=2,\;3$

#### **Q2**[JEE Main][discriminant]Find the nature of the roots of $2x^2-3x+5=0$.

**Answer:** $D=-31<0$; two non-real conjugate roots $\dfrac{3\pm i\sqrt{31}}{4}$

#### **Q3**[JEE Main][vieta]Find the sum and product of the roots of $3x^2-7x+2=0$.

**Answer:** sum $\dfrac73$, product $\dfrac23$

#### **Q4**[JEE Main][equal roots]Find the nature of the roots of $x^2-4x+4=0$.

**Answer:** $D=0$; one repeated root $x=2$

#### **Q5**[JEE Main][equal roots]Find $k$ so that $x^2+kx+9=0$ has equal roots.

**Answer:** $k=\pm6$

## B · Vieta and symmetric functions (Q6–Q10)

For $x^2-5x+6=0$ with roots $\alpha,\beta$:

#### **Q6**[JEE Main][symmetric]Find $\alpha^2+\beta^2$.

**Answer:** $13$

#### **Q7**[JEE Main][symmetric]Find $\alpha^3+\beta^3$.

**Answer:** $35$

#### **Q8**[JEE Main][symmetric]Find $\dfrac1\alpha+\dfrac1\beta$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac56$

#### **Q9**[JEE Main][symmetric]Find $\alpha^2\beta+\alpha\beta^2$.

**Answer:** $30$

#### **Q10**[JEE Main][symmetric]Find $(\alpha-\beta)^2$ and $\lvert\alpha-\beta\rvert$.

**Answer:** $1$ and $1$

## C · The quadratic expression (Q11–Q15)

#### **Q11**[JEE Main][minimum]Find the minimum of $x^2-6x+11$ and where it occurs.

**Answer:** minimum $2$ at $x=3$

#### **Q12**[JEE Main][maximum]Find the maximum of $-x^2+4x-1$ and where it occurs.

**Answer:** maximum $3$ at $x=2$

#### **Q13**[JEE Main][sign]Find the sign of $x^2-x-6$ at $x=0$.

**Answer:** negative ($-6$)

#### **Q14**[JEE Main][sign]Solve $x^2-5x+6<0$.

**Answer:** $2<x<3$

#### **Q15**[JEE Adv][amgm]Find the least value of $x+\dfrac4x$ for $x>0$.

**Answer:** $4$, attained at $x=2$

## D · Equations reducible to quadratics (Q16–Q20)

#### **Q16**[JEE Main][biquadratic]Solve $x^4-5x^2+4=0$.

**Answer:** $x=\pm1,\;\pm2$

#### **Q17**[JEE Main][nested]Solve $(x+1)^2=4(x+1)$.

**Answer:** $x=-1,\;3$

#### **Q18**[JEE Adv][radical]Solve $\sqrt{x+3}=x-3$.

**Answer:** $x=6$ only ($x=1$ is extraneous)

#### **Q19**[JEE Adv][reciprocal]Solve $x^2+\dfrac1{x^2}=7$.

**Answer:** $x=\dfrac{3\pm\sqrt5}{2}$ and $x=\dfrac{-3\pm\sqrt5}{2}$

#### **Q20**[JEE Main][exponential]Solve $2^{2x}-5\cdot2^x+4=0$.

**Answer:** $x=0,\;2$

## E · Common roots and transformed equations (Q21–Q25)

#### **Q21**[JEE Main][common]Find the common root of $x^2-5x+6=0$ and $x^2-4x+3=0$.

**Answer:** $x=3$

#### **Q22**[JEE Adv][common condition]State the condition for a common root of $x^2+px+q=0$ and $x^2+rx+s=0$.

**Answer:** $(p-r)^2=(q-s)(r-p)$, or subtract to get $x=\dfrac{s-q}{p-r}$

#### **Q23**[JEE Main][construct]Form the equation whose roots are $2+\sqrt3$ and $2-\sqrt3$.

**Answer:** $x^2-4x+1=0$

#### **Q24**[JEE Main][reciprocal]Form the equation whose roots are the reciprocals of the roots of $x^2-5x+6=0$.

**Answer:** $6x^2-5x+1=0$

#### **Q25**[JEE Main][shift]Form the equation whose roots are $\alpha+1$ and $\beta+1$, where $\alpha,\beta$ are the roots of $x^2-5x+6=0$.

**Answer:** $x^2-7x+12=0$

## F · The parabola and location of roots (Q26–Q27)

#### **Q26**[JEE Main][vertex]Find the vertex and axis of $y=2x^2-8x+5$.

**Answer:** vertex $(2,-3)$, axis $x=2$

#### **Q27**[JEE Adv][location]Find the range of $k$ for which both roots of $x^2-2kx+k^2-1=0$ exceed $1$.

**Answer:** $k>2$

## G · Olympiad inequalities (Q28–Q31)

#### **Q28**[JEE Adv][nesbitt]Prove that $\dfrac{a}{b+c}+\dfrac{b}{c+a}+\dfrac{c}{a+b}\ge\dfrac32$ for $a,b,c>0$.

**Answer:** proved; equality at $a=b=c$

#### **Q29**[JEE Main][amgm]Find the least value of $x+\dfrac1x$ for $x>0$.

**Answer:** $2$, attained at $x=1$

#### **Q30**[JEE Adv][reciprocal sum]Prove that $(a+b+c)\left(\dfrac1a+\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c\right)\ge9$ for $a,b,c>0$.

**Answer:** proved; equality at $a=b=c$

#### **Q31**[Olympiad][titu]Prove that $\dfrac{a^2}{b}+\dfrac{c^2}{d}\ge\dfrac{(a+c)^2}{b+d}$ for $b,d>0$.

**Answer:** proved by Cauchy–Schwarz; equality when $\dfrac ab=\dfrac cd$

## H · Olympiad frontier (Q32–Q34)

#### **Q32**[JEE Adv][identity]Factorise $a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc$, and state the consequence when $a+b+c=0$.

**Answer:** $(a+b+c)(a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca)$; hence $a^3+b^3+c^3=3abc$

#### **Q33**[Olympiad][schur]Verify Schur's inequality $a(a-b)(a-c)+b(b-c)(b-a)+c(c-a)(c-b)\ge0$ for $(a,b,c)=(1,2,3)$ and $(3,4,5)$.

**Answer:** $6$ and $12$ — both non-negative ✓

#### **Q34**[JEE Adv][cubic]Solve $x^3-6x^2+11x-6=0$.

**Answer:** $x=1,\;2,\;3$

> [!note] Exam technique notes
> - Reach for Vieta before solving: most questions ask for a *function of the
>   roots*, not the roots themselves.
> - Always state the discriminant before the roots; it costs one line and often
>   answers the question by itself.
> - After squaring, substitute back — extraneous roots are the commonest lost
>   mark in this topic.
> - For inequalities, check positivity of every variable before quoting AM–GM,
>   and identify the equality case.
