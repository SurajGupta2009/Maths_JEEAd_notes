PRINTING — Short Notes: 01-sets-relations-functions
===================================================

Sheet size: A4 portrait (210 x 297 mm). Every PNG is exactly 2480 x 3508 px
at nominal 300 DPI = one A4 page.

RECOMMENDED (bookmarked PDFs, one sheet per page)
  - Combined (JEE first, then Olympiad):
      exports/01-sets-relations-functions-combined.pdf
  - JEE only:          exports/01-sets-relations-functions-JEE.pdf
  - Extensions only:   exports/01-sets-relations-functions-Olympiad-extensions.pdf

Print settings (any viewer: Adobe Reader, Preview, Chrome, evince):
  - Paper size: A4, orientation: PORTRAIT
  - Scaling: "Fit" / "Fit to page" / "Shrink oversized pages"
    -> NOT "Fill", NOT "Actual size", NOT "Crop", NOT "Poster"
  - One sheet per page (do not impose multiple sheets per side)
  - Colour printing recommended (yellow key results / red traps carry meaning);
    greyscale remains fully readable
  - Bookmarks in the PDFs give the sheet order; print in document order

FROM THE PNGs DIRECTLY
  ZIPs: exports/01-sets-relations-functions-combined-sheets.zip (JEE first),
  -JEE-sheets.zip, -Olympiad-sheets.zip. Each PNG is one page: load 01.png,
  02.png, ... in numeric order (JEE/ folder before Olympiad/ in the combined
  zip), print with "Fit to page". Do not let the printer driver crop to
  printable area at 100% without "fit" - the sheets carry 10 mm safe margins,
  but "Fill" will still crop them.

REBUILDING FROM SOURCE
  python3 scripts/render.py && python3 scripts/export.py
  (see README.md; editable sources are in content/)
